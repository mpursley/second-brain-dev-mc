# **Compensation & Equity Projection: Founder/CEO (Matt Pursley)**

This document models two distinct paths for the Founder/CEO’s equity and cash accumulation over a 3-year period. Both scenarios assume a 1-year cliff and a 3-year total vest to align with VC expectations.

**Common Assumptions:**

* **Total Authorized Shares:** 10,000,000.  
* **Vesting Schedule:** 3 Years (36 Months), 1-Year Cliff (12 Months).  
* **Salary Trigger:** $145,000/year (Starts upon successful $3M Pre-Seed funding).  
* **Funding Event:** $3M Pre-Seed round closes at the start of Month 1\.

| Metric | Option 1: Equal Split | Option 2: High Control |

## **Strategic Notes for the Founder**

1. **The "Controlling Interest" Threshold:** In Option 2, you enter a Series A round owning over 50% of the company even after dilution from the $3M pre-seed. This is a massive strategic advantage for board control and future decision-making.  
2. **Room for a CTO:** Option 2 specifically leaves 3,000,000 shares (30%) unallocated to you. This is enough to give a COO 12.5%, a CTO 12.5%, and still have a 5% pool for early "Director-level" AI engineer hires.  
3. **Resetting the Clock:** Both options assume you reset your vesting. While you already own 8M shares, investors will likely require you to "re-vest" these shares over 3 years to ensure you don't leave the company immediately after taking their $3M.  
4. **Tax Considerations (Section 83(b)):** If you move to either of these new structures, you must file a new 83(b) election for the re-issued/re-vested shares to avoid a massive tax bill when the company valuation jumps from $0 to $30M.
