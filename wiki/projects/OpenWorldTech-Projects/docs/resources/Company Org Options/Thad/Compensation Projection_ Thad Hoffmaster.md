# **Compensation & Equity Projection: Thad Hoffmaster**

This document models the projected cash and equity accumulation over a 3-year period for the two engagement paths proposed to Thad Hoffmaster.

**Assumptions:**

* **Total Authorized Shares:** 10,000,000.  
* **Vesting Schedule:** 3 Years (36 Months), 1-Year Cliff (12 Months).  
* **Funding Event:** $3M Pre-Seed round closes at the start of Month 1\.  
* **Equity Calculation:** Based on a 10M share base (fully diluted percentages will decrease slightly upon SAFE conversion).

## **Scenario A: Founding Advisor & Board Member**

*Focus: Strategic guidance and fundraising support (10-20 hrs/month).*

| Milestone | Cash (Retainer) | Equity (1.5% Grant) | Cumulative Shares Vested |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Year 1 (Months 1-12)** | $36,000 ($3k/mo) | 0.5% (at Month 12 Cliff) | 50,000 |
| **Year 2 (Months 13-24)** | $36,000 ($3k/mo) | 0.5% (vesting monthly) | 100,000 |
| **Year 3 (Months 25-36)** | $36,000 ($3k/mo) | 0.5% (vesting monthly) | 150,000 |
| **Total 3-Year Value** | **$108,000** | **1.5% Ownership** | **150,000** |

## **Scenario B: Full-Time Co-Founder & COO**

*Focus: Operational leadership, scaling, and full-time execution.*

| Milestone | Cash (Salary) | Equity (12.5% Grant) | Cumulative Shares Vested |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Year 1 (Months 1-12)** | $145,000 | 4.16% (at Month 12 Cliff) | 416,667 |
| **Year 2 (Months 13-24)** | $145,000 | 4.16% (vesting monthly) | 833,334 |
| **Year 3 (Months 25-36)** | $145,000 | 4.16% (vesting monthly) | 1,250,000 |
| **Total 3-Year Value** | **$435,000** | **12.5% Ownership** | **1,250,000** |

## **Vesting Visual (Monthly Percentage)**

* **Months 1 \- 11:** 0% (Pre-Cliff phase).  
* **Month 12 (The Cliff):** 33.3% of total grant vests immediately.  
* **Months 13 \- 36:** 2.77% of total grant vests each month until fully vested at Month 36\.
