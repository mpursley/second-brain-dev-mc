# Tech Startup Pitch Deck Template

## 1. Title / Intro
- Company Name
- Logo
- Tagline / One-sentence value proposition
- Presenter Name & Title / Contact Info

## 2. Problem
- Describe the core problem you are solving.
- Who is experiencing this problem?
- Why is it a significant pain point?

## 3. Solution
- How does your company solve the problem?
- Value proposition for the customer.

## 4. Product
- Overview of the product/service.
- Key features and functionalities.
- Screenshots, architecture, or demo links.

## 5. Market Size / Opportunity
- Total Addressable Market (TAM).
- Serviceable Available Market (SAM).
- Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM).
- Target audience / Customer persona.

## 6. Business Model
- How do you make money? (Pricing strategy, SaaS, one-time, marketplace, etc.)
- Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) vs. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) expectations.

## 7. Traction / Milestones
- Key milestones achieved to date.
- User growth, revenue, partnerships, or product development progress.

## 8. Competition / Competitive Advantage
- Who are your direct and indirect competitors?
- Your "secret sauce" or competitive moat.
- Positioning map or feature comparison table.

## 9. Go-To-Market Strategy
- How will you reach your target customers?
- Marketing channels, sales strategy, current distribution paths.

## 10. Team
- Core team members and their roles.
- Relevant background, past successes, and domain expertise.

## 11. Financial Projections
- 3 to 5-year high-level financial forecast.
- Projected revenue, major expenses, and profitability targets.

## 12. The Ask / Funding
- How much capital are you raising?
- What are the primary uses of funds? (e.g., engineering, marketing, sales)
- Next milestones you will achieve with this funding.
