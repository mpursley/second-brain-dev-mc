## 3. The Script (Slides 1-16)

### Slide 1: Title (0:30)
> "Hi everyone, I'm Matt Pursley, the founder of OpenWorldTech. Over my career, I've built and managed massive, highly scalable distributed systems for companies like Sony and Improbable. Today, I'm building OpenWorldTech to bring that exact same frontier intelligence and engineering rigor to everyday businesses and users."

### Slide 2: The Problem (1:00)
> "Right now, AI is incredibly powerful, but it's siloed. It lacks context. Whether you are an Enterprise struggling with fragmented cloud observability, or an individual trying to manage your personal productivity, the pain point is the same: you want powerful intelligence, but you need it to be private, and you need it to actually execute on your specific data."

### Slide 3: The Solution (1:00)
> "OpenWorldTech solves this with a dual-pronged ecosystem. On the B2B side, we have The Genius Suite—bringing autonomous site reliability and DevOps to the enterprise. On the B2C side, we have Assistant, PhD and The Creative Suite. We call it 'Insights for for Your Life'—treating personal habits and workflows with the same robust architecture we use to manage global server infrastructure."

### Slide 4: Why Now? (0:45)
> "Timing is everything. Frontier AI is finally accessible locally. We are shifting from simple chatbots to autonomous agentic workflows capable of multi-step execution. And most importantly, trust in Big Tech is eroding; the market is demanding sovereign, privacy-first AI."

### Slide 5: Market Size (0:45)
> "We are operating at the intersection of Cloud Computing, AI SaaS, and Personal Productivity—a massive $850B+ market. But our immediate focus, our Serviceable Obtainable Market, is the $16B space of Enterprise SRE and DevOps automation pipelines."

### Slide 6: The Product Ecosystem (0:30)
> "Our ecosystem has three pillars: Assistant PhD for the mass market, The Creative Suite as a funnel for creators, and The Genius Suite for the enterprise."

### Slide 7: Assistant, PhD (0:45)
> "Assistant, PhD is our B2C hub. It’s an autonomous ecosystem built across 6 pillars of life management—from Vitality to Project Management. It runs on a token-based economy facilitating both Agent-to-Agent and Agent-to-Human physical world workflows."

### Slide 8: The Creative Suite (0:45)
> "To drive users into that ecosystem, we built The Creative Suite. These are high-demand, highly utility-driven tools like TouchUpMyPhoto and TouchUpMyVideo. Users come for a fast, AI-driven media edit, and stay when they realize the power of the broader platform."

### Slide 9: The Genius Suite (0:45)
> "For our B2B pipeline, The Genius Suite applies autonomous intelligence to real-world engineering. This includes automated incident response via Genius-SRE, Terraform generation via Genius-DevOps, and automated code review via Genius-SWE."

### Slide 10: Go-To-Market (1:00)
> "Our Go-To-Market strategy is highly pragmatic. We use The Creative Suite as a top-of-funnel utility magnet for B2C acquisition. Simultaneously, we leverage our network to secure high-margin B2B integration consulting, which immediately bootstraps the R&D for our scalable products."

### Slide 11: Competitive Advantage (0:45)
> "Our moat is our privacy architecture. We offer a 'Bring Your Own AI' approach, running local models via Ollama or independent APIs. We don't train on user data. We simply provide the SRE-inspired dashboard to analyze it."

### Slide 12: Traction (0:45)
> "We are moving fast. We have secured our core domains, architected the 'Possibility Engine' UI, mapped the 6 pillars, and defined the strategic pipeline to transition our initial cloud consultancy into these scalable AI suites."

### Slide 13: Business Models (0:30)
> "Revenue comes from three streams: B2B consulting retainers, the multi-token B2C economy powering Agent execution, and traditional SaaS subscription services for our Creative Suite tools."

### Slide 14: Financial Projections (0:45)
> "Because of our B2B consulting pipeline, we forecast reaching $300k in gross revenue in Year 1, allowing us to self-fund our beta launches. By Year 2, as the product suites scale, we project $750k in revenue with high gross margins."

### Slide 15: The Team (0:30)
> "I bring deep technical execution risk down to zero. I’ve built the CI/CD pipelines, observability stacks, and infrastructure for massive global platforms. We anticipate using this round to make our first strategic engineering and growth hires to complement this foundation."

### Slide 16: The Ask (1:00)
> "To accelerate this, we are raising a $1M Pre-Seed round. We are optimizing for speed and rewarding early conviction, so we've structured this in two tiers using standard YC SAFEs. The first $500k allocation is open right now at a heavily discounted $4M post-money cap, with the second close at an $8M cap. This capital goes directly toward scaling our multi-token backend, expanding our AI integration team, and executing our user acquisition strategy. I’d love to answer any questions you have."
