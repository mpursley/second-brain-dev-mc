# OpenWorldTech.io - Profit & Loss (P&L) Statement Forecast
*Note: This is a forward-looking pro forma projection for internal planning and investor context.*

## Year 1 (Months 1-12) - The Bootstrap Phase

### 1. Revenue
*   **Product/SaaS Revenue (Assistant-PhD Beta / Early Access):** $100,000
    * (Targeting 500 Early Access Customers * $16.7/month * 12 months)
*   **Consulting Services (Retainers / Projects):** $200,000
    *   (Targeting 2-3 anchor clients, $16.7K/mo average)
*   **Total Gross Revenue:** $300,000

### 2. Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
*   **Hosting / Cloud Infrastructure (AWS/GCP/CoreWeave/LLM API):** $25,000 (R&D and internal tooling)
*   **Software Licenses (GitHub, Slack, Atlassian, AI APIs):** $5,000
*   **Total COGS:** $30,000

### 3. Gross Profit
*   **Gross Profit:** $270,000 (90% Gross Margin)

### 4. Operating Expenses (OpEx)
*   **Payroll & Benefits (Founder Draw / Base):** $120,000
*   **Contractors (Design, specialized dev):** $30,000
*   **Marketing & Sales (Travel, Conferences, Ads):** $10,000
*   **Legal & Professional Services (Clerky, CPA, Insurance):** $10,000
*   **General & Administrative (Office, Internet, Supplies):** $30,000
*   **Total Operating Expenses:** $200,000

### 5. Net Income (EBITDA)
*   **Net Income Year 1 Forecast:** $70,000 (Reinvested into R&D and team scaling)

## Year 2 (Months 13-24) - Product Scaling Phase

### 1. Revenue
*   **Consulting Services (Expanded Team):** $600,000
*   **Product/SaaS Revenue (Assistant-PhD Subscriptions & CreativeSuite Usage):** $150,000
*   **Total Gross Revenue:** $750,000

### 2. Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
*   **Hosting / Cloud Infrastructure (Scaling Products):** $60,000

*   **Total COGS:** $75,000

### 3. Gross Profit
*   **Gross Profit:** $675,000 (90% Gross Margin)

### 4. Operating Expenses (OpEx)
*   **Payroll & Benefits (Founder + 2-3 key engineering hires):** $400,000
*   **Contractors / Freelance:** $40,000
*   **Marketing & Sales (Expanded budget, GDC/Re:Invent):** $35,000
*   **Legal & Professional Services:** $15,000
*   **General & Administrative:** $10,000
*   **Total Operating Expenses:** $500,000

### 5. Net Income (EBITDA)
*   **Net Income Year 2 Forecast:** $175,000

## Summary Notes
*   **High Margin Focus:** Early reliance on B2B consulting yields high margins to self-fund the R&D burn for the Assistant-PhD consumer platform and Game Server Ops products.
*   **Conservative SaaS Projections:** Product revenue is modeled conservatively in Year 1 as the focus is on robust beta testing and product-market fit.
*   **Reinvestment:** Profits in Years 1 and 2 are primarily targeted for reinvestment into engineering talent and scaling infrastructure limits (particularly GPU compute for AI integration).
