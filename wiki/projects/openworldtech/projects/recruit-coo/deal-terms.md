# COO Recruitment: Preferred vs. Acceptable Deal Terms

This document outlines the target compensation and governance terms for the Chief Operating Officer (COO) position at Open World Tech Inc. (Delaware).

## Summary Table

| Term | Preferred (Target) | Acceptable (Floor) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Title** | COO & Co-Founder | COO |
| **Equity** | 1.5% – 3.0% (Post-Money) | 0.75% – 1.25% (Post-Money) |
| **Salary** | $200k – $225k (Market Rate) | $165k – $185k (Equity Trade-off) |
| **Bonus** | 20% Performance-based | 10% Performance-based |
| **Vesting** | Standard 4-year, 1-year cliff | 4-year, 6-month cliff or 3-year |
| **Acceleration** | Double-trigger (Change of Control) | Single-trigger (Partial) |

---

## Detailed Breakdown

### 1. Equity & Title
*   **Preferred:** **1.5% - 3.0%** equity grant as a Co-Founder. This reflects a "Day 1" or early-stage "Integrator" role common in AI Productivity startups where the COO is expected to build the operational infrastructure from scratch.
*   **Acceptable:** **0.75% - 1.25%** for a professional COO hire (non-founder status) with significant scaling experience.

### 2. Cash Compensation (Salary & Bonus)
*   **Preferred:** **$200k - $225k base** plus a 20% performance bonus. AI and SRE startups often pay a premium (15-20%) due to technical complexity and high demand for operational talent who can manage GPU/cloud infrastructure costs.
*   **Acceptable:** **$165k - $185k base** if coupled with the upper end of the equity range (e.g., >2%).

### 3. Vesting & Acceleration
*   **Preferred:** **Standard 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff**. Includes "Double-Trigger" acceleration (100% vesting if the company is acquired AND the COO is terminated without cause within 12 months).
*   **Acceptable:** **4-year vesting with a shorter cliff (6 months)** or a 3-year vesting schedule to attract top-tier talent from competitive AI labs. "Single-trigger" acceleration for 25-50% of unvested equity upon change of control.

### 4. Role Scope (AI & SRE Focus)
The COO is expected to lead:
*   **Infrastructure Strategy:** Managing cloud/GPU partnerships and cost optimization for "SRE for Life" services.
*   **Go-to-Market (GTM):** Scaling the AI Productivity suite to enterprise customers.
*   **Recruitment:** Scaling the engineering and operations teams.

### 5. Benefits & Perks
*   **Standard:** Health, Dental, Vision (100% covered for employee).
*   **Strategic:** Dedicated "Professional Development" budget for AI/SRE certifications and conferences.
