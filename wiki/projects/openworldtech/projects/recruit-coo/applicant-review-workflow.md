# Applicant Review Process

This document outlines the workflow for reviewing applications safely and effectively.

## Workflow
1. **Initial Review**: Screening against the job description requirements.
2. **NDA Signing**: If moving forward, require the candidate to sign the NDA.
3. **Internal Review**: Assess fit and preparation.
4. **Interview Scheduling**: Reach out with available times.
5. **Interview Execution**: Conduct the interview.
6. **Offer Generation**: Based on "Preferred vs Acceptable" terms.
