# **Meeting Summary: Matt Pursley & Todd Yancey**

**Date:** March 25, 2026

**Participants:** Matt Pursley (CEO, OpenWorldTech), Todd Yancey (Candidate/Advisor)

**Goal:** Introductory "Stealth Mode" briefing and product demo.

## **Executive Summary**

Todd Yancey validated the core concept of the "Genius Suite" and the "Assistant PhD" but provided a strong reality check regarding the scale of the project. His primary feedback is that the current fundraising ask is likely too low for the "ocean" Matt is attempting to boil, and the company needs to narrow its focus to a specific "Knowledge Worker" ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) to succeed.

## **Key Discussion Points**

### **1\. Product & Technology Review**

* **The Demo:** Matt shared the "Assistant PhD" and "Genius Suite" prototypes. Todd noted that while the front-ends look good, the project appears to be in the "Alpha" stage.  
* **Technical Leverage:** Matt explained the "AI-Leveraged" approach—using Gemini CLI and Claude Code to build infrastructure and front-ends with minimal human overhead.  
* **Data Privacy Moat:** Matt emphasized that the core value proposition is **security and privacy**. Unlike "Big Tech" (Gmail/OpenAI), OpenWorldTech will not sell user data.  
* **The Moat:** Todd questioned the "Moat" (defensibility). He noted that since anyone can use wrappers, the uniqueness must come from the **semantics and organization**, not just the underlying LLM.

### **2\. Strategic Feedback from Todd**

* **"Boiling the Ocean":** Todd cautioned that the current vision covers a massive amount of territory (Personal Assistant \+ Creative Suite \+ Enterprise SRE). He suggests focusing on one repeatable ICP first.  
* **Target Audience:** Todd suggested focusing strictly on **Knowledge Workers** within the Enterprise as the primary entry point, emphasizing productivity and data safety.  
* **Market Analogy:** Todd compared the current AI era to the birth of the PC—where individuals (like accountants) brought tech into the office for productivity before the corporation officially adopted it.

### **3\. Fundraising & Financials**

* **The Ask:** Todd explicitly stated that **$3M is not enough.**  
* **Recommendation:** He suggested that a project of this scale requires at least **$10M in Seed funding** to build a proper team and a "real" (non-alpha) solution.  
* **Investor Psychology:** Todd noted that VCs will want to see "Minimum Viable Proof" beyond just PowerPoint—ideally a killer demo and clear social proof/waitlists.

## **Critical Feedback & Risks**

* **The "Vibe Coding" Trap:** Todd warned that while automated/vibe coding is great for 0-to-1, you will eventually need a professional development team to handle semantics and scalability.  
* **Large Lab Competition:** There is a constant risk of OpenAI/Google releasing a "wrapper killer" within 6-12 months.

## **Next Steps & Action Items**

* **Pitch Deck Review:** Todd offered to review the pitch deck again and provide notes on how to "ask for more money."  
* **VC Networking:** Todd mentioned he is willing to talk to a few VCs in his network to gauge interest (noting that these VCs will *not* sign NDAs).  
* **Financial Calibration:** Matt to reconsider the $1M/$3M targets in favor of a larger $10M seed-style narrative to match the scope of the Genius Suite.

## **Overall Impression**

Todd is acting more like a **Strategic Advisor/Consulting CFO** at this stage. He is looking for a technical engine (Matt) to pair with his commercial/fundraising expertise. He seems very interested in the "Alternative Capital" angle—using his background to bridge the gap between technical vision and institutional funding.
