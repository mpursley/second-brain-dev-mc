# Meeting Notes - Intro Call with Rakesh Pullabhatla

## Technical Discussion: Gemini Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)
* **Process:** Workflow for Gemini 1.5 (Pro/Flash) on Vertex AI using JSONL data in Cloud Storage.
* **Best Practices:** Use distinct training and validation sets to prevent overfitting.
* **Hyperparameters:** Control over epochs, adapter size, and learning rate.
* **Costs:** Estimated at ~$5 per 1 million training tokens. A 1,000-example dataset (~1M tokens) with 3-5 epochs costs roughly $15-$30.
* **Optimization:** Recommended to turn off the "thinking budget" for SFT models to reduce latency and output token costs without sacrificing accuracy.
