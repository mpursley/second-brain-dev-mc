We are building a dual-pronged AI ecosystem designed to solve the complexity of modern life and enterprise infrastructure. Our mission is to engineer intelligent products that harness dynamic context and deep data to drive measurable impact for every Business and every User.

Our founder brings 20+ years of engineering leadership from top-tier global platforms:

- Fintech: Zepz.io/Sendwave/WorldRemit, Sage.com

We have the technical depth to build at massive scale. Now, we are looking for an operational co-founder to help us turn this vision into a funded, category-defining company.

* **Media Utility Suite (B2C):** High-utility media tools that serve as top-of-funnel magnets for our broader ecosystem.
