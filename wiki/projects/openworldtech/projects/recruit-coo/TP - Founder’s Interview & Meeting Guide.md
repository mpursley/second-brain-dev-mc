# **Founder’s Meeting Guide: OpenWorldTech Intro Calls**

**Goal:** Assess mutual chemistry, strategic fit, and define the COO vs. Advisor path.

**Total Duration:** 60 Minutes

## **1\. Opening & The "Technical Truth" (0:00 \- 0:10)**

* **The Icebreaker:** "Thanks for jumping on. You’ve likely seen the background, but to be clear: I’m an Infra/SRE guy by trade. I’ve built the technical engine, but I’m jumping into the deep end on the 'Startup/CEO' mechanics. I’m looking for a partner who thrives in the company operations side, so I can focus on the systems, products, tools, and user satisfaction."  
* **Document Check:** "Were you able to review the Pitch Deck and Cap Table?  
  * *If Yes:* "Any immediate red flags or specific questions you want to hit first?"  
  * *If No:* "No worries. I’ll give you the 'Stealth' summary in 5 minutes then we can dive in."

## **2\. The Vision: Agentic AI Ecosystem (0:10 \- 0:20)**

* **The Role:** "I see myself as the 'AI Architect'—working with foundation models and agentic wrappers (Nemo, etc.) to build the tools. I want to build the 'Genius Stack' that I wish I had as an SRE."  
* **The Growth Strategy:** Mention the 'Magnet' strategy (Consumer Creative tools driving Enterprise SRE acquisition).  
* **The Tech Stack:** Discuss the leverage of **Agentic Wrappers**. We aren't just calling APIs; we are building autonomous workflows that allow a 3-person team to do the work of a 50-person engineering org.

## **3\. Founder Options (0:20 \- 0:35)**

* **The 4 Scenarios:** Present the options you've been weighing:  
  1. **Solo \+ AI Agents:** High effort, slow speed.  
  2. **Duo (CEO/COO):** Traditional, but potentially high-stress for two.  
  3. **Duo \+ Heavy Advisory:** Leverage network for the raise while we build.  
  4. **The Trio (CEO, COO, CPO/CTO):** The "Scenario D" model. High stability for a $1M raise.  
* **The "Ask":** "I want to be in the technology/infra/observability side. I need the partner who *enjoys* the daily ops: HR, legal, vendors, and the marketing engine. Which of those scenarios feels like the 'winning' play to you?"

## **4\. Fundraising Calibration (0:35 \- 0:45)**

* **The "Bar" for Investment:** "In your experience, what is the 'Minimum Viable Proof' we need to show investors to close this $1M?  
  * *Follow-up:* "Do we need a fully working demo, or is a 1,000-person waiting list and a blueprinted ecosystem enough?"  
  * *Follow-up:* "How much weight do you place on early ARR vs. top-of-funnel 'social proof' (e.g., YouTube views, waitlists) for an AI play right now?"  
* **Comparables:** Mention the **Vectal.ai ($1.8M exit)** and **Cal AI ($50M exit)** cases. "How do we frame our 'traction' to match or exceed those benchmarks?"

## **5\. Logistics & Next Steps (0:45 \- 1:00)**

* **COO vs. Advisor:** "If the full-time equity-only sprint isn't the right fit today, would you be interested in the **Advisory Board** role? It’s low-friction (2-4 hours/mo) focused on intros and strategy."
* **Closing:** "I’ll let you sit with the Data Room. Let’s sync via email on whether we move to a 'Product Deep Dive' or an Advisory setup."
### **Key "Matt-isms" to use during the call:**

* *"I'd rather own a smaller piece of a giant pie than 100% of a grape."* (Explaining the 29% equity split).  
* *"We are building a company that uses its own product."* (Explaining why we stay lean).  
* *"The LLMs might eventually try to do my job too, that's why we're building the wrappers now."*
