## The Opportunity

At **OpenWorldTech Inc.**, we are building a dual-pronged AI ecosystem designed to solve the complexity of modern life and enterprise infrastructure. Our mission is to engineer intelligent products that harness dynamic context and deep data to drive measurable impact for every Business and every User.

Our founder, **Matt Pursley**, brings 20+ years of engineering leadership from **Sony PlayStation (PS Now)**, **Improbable Worlds (Metaverse Infrastructure)**, and **Zepz (Fintech/WorldRemit)**. We have the technical depth to build at massive scale. Now, we are looking for an operational co-founder to help us turn this vision into a funded, category-defining company.

## Our Ecosystem

*   **The Genius Suite (B2B):** Cloud-native solutions, unified observability, and AI/LLM safety red-teaming for enterprises struggling with cloud complexity.
*   **The Assistant, PhD (B2C):** An "SRE for Your Life" autonomous intelligence platform that treats personal habits, wellness, and workflows with the same rigor used to manage global technical infrastructure.
*   **The Creative Suite (B2C):** High-utility media tools (Touch Up My Photo/Video/etc.) that serve as top-of-funnel magnets for our broader ecosystem.
