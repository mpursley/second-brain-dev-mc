## California-Specific Information

If you plan on issuing securities to service providers located in California, you should look into making the securities filing required under Section 25102(o) of the California Corporations Code (commonly referred to as a "25102(o) notice"). This must be done no later than 30 days of the first issuance from the stock plan to someone in California. You can do this online through the Self-Service DOCQNET Portal found on the California Department of Financial Protection and Innovation (DFPI) website: https://docqnet.dfpi.ca.gov/.

Since the online 25102(o) filing process can be confusing, we’ve obtained the information below from the DFPI to help guide you in this process. You can also find additional information on the DFPI website: https://dfpi.ca.gov/securities-and-franchises-frequently-asked-questions-and-answers/#3.
