# Job Description: AI-First Fullstack Software Engineer

## Role Overview
We are looking for a visionary **AI-First Fullstack Software Engineer** to join our team. In this role, you won't just be "using AI"—you will be architecting systems where AI is a core component of the user experience and the development lifecycle. You will bridge the gap between traditional fullstack engineering and the rapidly evolving world of Large Language Models (LLMs), specifically leveraging the Google Gemini ecosystem.

Our ideal candidate views AI as a fundamental primitive. You are comfortable building robust, scalable web applications and CLI tools while simultaneously engineering sophisticated prompt chains, agent-to-agent (A2A) workflows, and integrating with the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

## Key Responsibilities
*   **AI-Native Product Development:** Design and implement features that leverage the Google Gemini API (`@google/genai`) to solve complex user problems, focusing on intuitive AI-human interaction.
*   **Fullstack Engineering:** Build and maintain high-performance web applications using **React 19**, **Vite**, and **Tailwind CSS 4**.
*   **Backend & Bot Services:** Develop and optimize backend services using **Node.js (Express)** and **Go** (for high-performance bot services on Telegram/WhatsApp).
*   **AI Orchestration:** Explore and implement experimental agentic frameworks and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to enhance LLM capabilities.
*   **Prompt Engineering:** Design, test, and iterate on complex prompt architectures to ensure reliable, high-quality AI outputs.
*   **Infrastructure & DevOps:** Deploy and manage applications using **Docker**, **Kubernetes (K8s)**, and **ArgoCD** on **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**.
*   **Continuous Innovation:** Stay at the bleeding edge of AI research, specifically within the Gemini and MCP ecosystems.

## Technical Requirements
*   **Frontend:** Mastery of **TypeScript**, **React 19**, and **Tailwind CSS 4**. Experience with **Ink** for building interactive CLI interfaces is a plus.
*   **Backend:** Strong proficiency in **Node.js/TypeScript** and **Go**. Experience designing RESTful APIs and working with bot frameworks.
*   **Databases:** Proficiency with **SQLite** (specifically `better-sqlite3` and Go drivers).
*   **AI Tooling:** Hands-on experience with LLM agent systems and AI coding assistants like **Google Gemini**, **Anthropic Claude Code/Cowork**, and **OpenAI Codex**. Deep understanding of the **Google Gemini API**, Model Context Protocol (MCP), and building agentic workflows.
*   **Cloud/DevOps:** Strong experience with **Kubernetes (K8s)**, **ArgoCD**, **Kind** (for local development), and **GCP (Cloud Run/Cloud Build)**.
*   **Tooling:** Familiarity with **Vite**, **Vitest**, and **esbuild**.

## Preferred Qualifications
*   Background in AI/ML or a deep personal portfolio of AI-powered projects.
*   Experience building "agentic" systems or agent-to-agent (A2A) communication protocols.
*   Contributions to open-source AI or web development projects.
*   Experience with automated infrastructure management (GitOps via ArgoCD).

## Our Culture: AI-First Mindset
We don't just build AI products; we work AI-first. 
*   **Leverage:** We are heavy users of LLM agent systems—including **Google Gemini**, **Anthropic Claude Code/Cowork**, and **OpenAI Codex**—to multiply our individual impact and accelerate development.
*   **Speed:** We value rapid prototyping and "shipping to learn" using modern toolchains like Vite and Go.
*   **Curiosity:** We are obsessed with the "what's next" in the AI space, particularly the evolution of Gemini, MCP, and autonomous coding agents.
*   **Agency:** We empower engineers to own products end-to-end, from K8s deployment to frontend polish.
