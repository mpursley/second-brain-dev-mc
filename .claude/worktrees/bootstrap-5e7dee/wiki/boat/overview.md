# Boat

Trailered, used in **salt water**, kept at the house between trips.

Procedures — the things to remember each time:

- [Parking it](parking.md)
- [Picking it up](pickup.md)
- [Launching it](launching.md)
- [Rinsing and cleaning it](rinsing-and-cleaning.md)

## Trailer lights

Not mounted on the trailer. They are **custom-made lines** that plug into the
truck and run to the back of the boat, so they **never go into the salt water**.

The trade-off is that they are exposed when the boat sits at the house, so they
have to be **stored inside the boat while parked** — see [parking](parking.md).

**Known failure mode: rain water getting into a light while parked.** One signal
light failed this way on 2026-08-15. The spare went on the same day,
which means there is currently **no spare** — building a replacement set is
[on the list](../../todo/life-stuff.md).

## Electrical

A **solar panel** and **two batteries**, with a **power switch** between them.

As of 2026-08-16 the panel charges only one battery — whichever the switch has
selected. The plan is to wire it to both so it trickle-charges both.

**Unconfirmed:** that the switch being set to `off` is what prevents the panel
reaching both batteries. That is the owner's working theory, untested. The make
and model of the switch is not recorded, and it matters — how a battery switch
routes a charge source depends on the type.
