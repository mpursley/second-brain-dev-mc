# Parking the boat

What to remember when the [boat](overview.md) goes back to the house.

## Steps

- [ ] **Bring the trailer light lines inside the boat.** They are custom lines
      that plug into the truck, not trailer-mounted, so they sit exposed once
      the boat is parked. Left out, rain gets into them — that is how a signal
      light failed on 2026-08-15.

## To fill in

parking — cover, battery switch, fuel, drain plug, chocks, straps — belongs
