# Launching the boat

At the ramp, putting the [boat](overview.md) in.

## Steps

- [ ] **Keep the trailer light lines out of the salt water.** The whole reason
      they are custom lines running from the truck to the back of the boat,
      rather than mounted on the trailer, is that the trailer goes in and they
      do not.
