# daily/

One note per day: `daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Created on demand by `/digest` or by
hand.

Holds the day's calendar, scratch thoughts, and anything captured but not yet
filed.

**Daily notes are not durable knowledge.** They are a staging area. Anything
worth keeping gets promoted into a `wiki/` page by `/ingest`, and the daily note
links to it. Do not go looking for facts in here — look in `wiki/`.

Old dailies are kept rather than deleted; they are cheap and occasionally useful
for reconstructing when something happened.
