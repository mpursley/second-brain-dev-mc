# Laminr is not the "Stealth Startup"

## Stated by the owner, 2026-08-15

Asked directly whether Laminr was the unnamed "Stealth Startup" his résumé
lists for Jan 2026 to present:

> no, different company

## What this corrects

An inference recorded earlier the same day in
`raw/work/2026-08-15-laminr-observations.md` and carried onto
`wiki/entities/laminr.md` and `wiki/people/matt-pursley.md`. It was marked
`Unconfirmed` on both pages rather than asserted, and it was wrong.

The reasoning that produced it, for the record: the dates fitted, and the
résumé's description of the stealth-startup role — CI/CD for web, desktop and
mobile apps shipping to Google Play, Apple, Steam and Epic — matched the
`Focus` and `Hunger` task lists almost item for item. Two things being similar
is not the same as being the same thing.

## What follows

Laminr and the stealth startup are two separate concerns, both current as of
2026-08-15. The résumé names only the latter.

The affiliation of `Site - Hunger` and `Site - Focus` is **still unknown**, and
the earlier hint now points away from Laminr rather than towards it: the
mobile-store pipeline they resemble is described under the stealth-startup entry,
not under anything connected to Laminr.

Not established: which of the two the owner spends most time on, whether either
is his own venture, and how the résumé's "Strategic Direct Placements
(Full-Time Embedded)" heading — which sits directly beneath the stealth-startup
block — relates to Laminr. That layout may be meaningful or may be an artifact
of how the résumé is organised. It has not been asked about.
