# Boat notes, 2026-08-16

Told by the owner on the morning of 2026-08-16, with the boat at the house.

## Trailer lights

The trailer lights are on **custom-made lines** that plug into the truck and run
to the back of the boat, rather than being mounted on the trailer itself. The
reason: **they never go into the salt water** that way.

They have to be **kept inside the boat while it is parked**, so they do not sit
out in the rain.

One of the signal lights **stopped working** on **2026-08-15**, while the boat
was parked. Suspected cause, in the owner's words,
"probably got too much rain water in it."

He had a spare set and **fitted it** the same day. That leaves him without a
spare, so a new working spare set needs to be built.

## Solar and batteries

The boat has a **solar panel** and **two batteries**, with a **power switch**
between them.

Planned work: **run wire from the solar panel to both batteries**, so the panel
trickle-charges both rather than one.

His working theory, stated as a belief rather than a fact:

> "i think with the power switch is set to 'off' it wont let the solar panel
> charge both the batteries... just the one its currently attached to"

**Unconfirmed.** Not tested, and the make and model of the battery switch is not
recorded.
